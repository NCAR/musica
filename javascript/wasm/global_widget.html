MUSICA WASM — Global Surface Chemistry

MICM solves a NOx–O3 mechanism independently in every cell of a global surface grid. A prescribed, non-divergent wind field then transports the species between the cells. Choose a flow pattern to see how the circulation shapes the plumes. All of the work happens in WebAssembly in this browser tab.
